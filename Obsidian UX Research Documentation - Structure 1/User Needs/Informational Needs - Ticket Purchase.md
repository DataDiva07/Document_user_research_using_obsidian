# Informational Needs

1. The ticket purchaser needs to know clear, multilingual guidance on how to use the ticket counter or vending machine interface (information) in order to complete a transaction without making errors (intended outcome).
2. The traveler needs to know how to identify available discounts, such as student discounts or regional passes like Bayern-Ticket (information), in order to make cost-effective choices (intended outcome).
3. The passenger needs to know whether the selected tickets match their intended destination and time (information) in order to ensure smooth travel (intended outcome).**
