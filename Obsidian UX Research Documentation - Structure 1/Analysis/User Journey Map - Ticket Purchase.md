# User Journey Map

| Stage                   | Actions                                 | Touchpoints          | Emotions            | Pain Points                                      | Opportunities                                           |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Awareness               | Identifies need for a ticket.           | Peer advice, online  | Confused 🤷🏻‍♀️ 🤔 | Lack of clear options for beginners.             | Provide a beginner-friendly ticketing FAQ/guide.        |
| Decision-<br><br>Making | Chooses platform (counter, app, kiosk). | Online, friends      | Hesitant 😐         | Uncertainty about the best option.               | Offer multilingual comparisons of purchase methods.     |
| Interaction             | Engages with chosen platform.           | Counter, kiosk, app  | Frustrated 😣       | Language barriers, confusing UI.                 | Train staff in language support; improve app usability. |
| Completion              | Acquires a ticket and verifies details. | App, physical ticket | Relieved 😮‍💨😌    | Errors in ticket selection or payment processes. | Automate ticket verification for accuracy.              |
