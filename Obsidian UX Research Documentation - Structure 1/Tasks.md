## Definition
A set of activities undertaken in order to achieve a specific goal. In general, tasks can be subdivided into Subtasks. 

These subtasks are supported by the future interactive system. For each subtask, the [[User Requirements - Ticket Purchase]] [[User Requirements - Traveling Across Cities]] specify the interaction required with the system, in terms of what the user must be able to recognize, select, or input. Subtasks either result from the initial task itself (“task-induced”) or from the specific implementation chosen for the interactive system (“system-induced”). System-induced subtasks are typically necessary but they do not contribute to the goal of the task.

## Examples of tasks and subtasks
- **Purchasing a train ticket** is a task.
- **Setting the language to English** is a (system-induced) subtask.
- **Selecting the correct ticket type** is a (task-induced) subtask.
- **Entering the destination** is a (task-induced) subtask.
- **Reviewing the selected ticket details** is a (task-induced) subtask.
- **Making the payment using a card** is a (system-induced) subtask.
- **Retrying card payment due to failure** is a (system-induced) subtask.
- **Validating the ticket before boarding** is a (task-induced) subtask.
- **Ensuring the ticket is valid for the journey** is a (task-induced) subtask.
