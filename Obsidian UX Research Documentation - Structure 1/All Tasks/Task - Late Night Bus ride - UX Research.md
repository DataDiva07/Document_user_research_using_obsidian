### Source / Link to Assumption node:
### Phase in task lifecycle: 

**Plan**
1. Identify the bus timetable for the next available night bus.
2. Determine the correct bus stop for her destination.
3. Assess whether her existing ticket is valid for the night bus.

**Prepare**
1. Access the timetable at the bus stop despite its confusing layout.
2. Seek clarity on the schedules for weekdays versus weekends.
3. Prepare a backup payment method in case her earlier ticket is invalid.

**Perform**
1. Board the bus and decide whether to validate her existing ticket or purchase a new one.
2. Observe the bus route to confirm the direction, despite dim lighting and the absence of a visible route map.
3. Consider seeking assistance from the bus driver or passengers, if necessary.

**Evaluate**
1. Verify the bus is traveling in the correct direction by recognizing landmarks or listening for stop announcements (if available).
2. Identify her stop and prepare to disembark once familiar surroundings appear.

**Communicate**
1. Share her concerns or feedback about the confusing schedules, dim lighting, and lack of route maps with peers or transportation authorities.
2. Offer tips to others in similar situations for navigating night bus travel.
