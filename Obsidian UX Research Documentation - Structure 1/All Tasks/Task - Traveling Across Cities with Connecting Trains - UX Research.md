### Phase in task lifecycle: 

#### Plan

1. Identify travel schedule and train connections using the DB Navigator app.
2. Check the duration of layovers between connections.
3. Verify train types (e.g., ICE or regional) for each segment.

#### Prepare

1. Save the digital ticket and itinerary details on her phone.
2. Ensure knowledge of platform numbers for transitions at each station.
3. Pack essentials for a comfortable journey (e.g., water, power bank).


#### Perform

1. Track real-time updates for any delays on her first train.
2. Locate platform numbers for the connecting train upon arrival in Hanover.
3. Board the connecting train in a timely manner and find her seat.

#### Evaluate

1. Confirm that her seat reservation (if applicable) is still valid on the connecting train.
2. Ensure she is on the correct train and heading to the intended destination.
3. Monitor real-time updates for any changes in the arrival time in Hamburg.

#### Communicate

1. Update a friend or colleague in Hamburg about her expected arrival time.
2. Seek assistance from staff or fellow passengers if platform changes or delays occur.
3. Notify peers or family if her arrival time changes significantly.
