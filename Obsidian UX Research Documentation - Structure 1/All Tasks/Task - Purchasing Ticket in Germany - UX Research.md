### Phase in task lifecycle: 

**Plan**
1. Identify travel requirements (destination, time, and type of travel).
2. Decide on a purchasing method (ticket counter, vending machine, or app).
3. Explore available discounts or promotions.

**Prepare**
1. Ensure access to a valid payment method (cash, card, or mobile payment).
2. Download required apps (e.g., DB Navigator).
3. Translate key ticketing terms for use at the kiosk or counter.

**Perform**
1. Navigate the chosen ticket-purchasing platform:
   - Access a kiosk, counter, or app.
   - Select the preferred language (if available).
   - Decide on the ticket provider.
2. Select travel details:
   - Enter destination, time, and ticket type.
   - Review pricing and available options.
3. Decide on a payment option:
   - Choose a suitable method (cash, card, mobile payment).
4. Review order:
   - Verify travel details, ticket type, and price.
5. Perform payment:
   - Execute payment according to instructions.
6. Retrieve ticket:
   - Obtain a physical or digital ticket.

**Evaluate**
1. Verify the ticket’s accuracy (destination, time, and validity).
2. Confirm compatibility with travel needs (e.g., valid for ICE or RE trains).

**Communicate**
1. Share ticket details with peers if needed for group travel.
2. Validate the ticket before boarding, if required.
