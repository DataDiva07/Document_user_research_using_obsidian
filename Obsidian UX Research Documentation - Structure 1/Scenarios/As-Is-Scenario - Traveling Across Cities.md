### As-Is Scenario

Title: Navigating Train Connections

Sara Das, a 23-year-old international student from India, is travelling from Berlin to Hamburg to visit a friend. Her journey involves a connection at Hanover. She books her ticket using the DB Navigator app and saves the details on her phone. On the day of travel, her train from Berlin to Hanover is delayed, reducing her layover time for the connecting train.

As Sara arrives in Hanover, she rushes to locate the connecting train platform. She notices a sudden platform change displayed on the station board. Flustered, she quickly gathers her luggage and moves to the updated platform. She reaches just in time to board her connecting train to Hamburg.

During the journey, she frequently checks the DB Navigator app to ensure the train is on schedule. Despite the initial stress, Sara successfully arrives in Hamburg, relieved but tired from the experience.
