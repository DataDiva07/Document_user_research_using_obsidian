

Sara’s Late-Night Bus Ride After attending a late-night university event, Sara decides to take a night bus home. She checks the timetable at the stop but finds the layout confusing, with different schedules for weekdays and weekends. The bus arrives later than expected, and Sara realizes she is unsure whether her ticket from earlier in the day is still valid. The dim lighting and lack of visible route maps inside the bus make her feel uneasy about whether she is heading in the right direction. She remains tense until she recognizes her stop and disembarks.
