# As-Is Scenario: Sara’s Ticket-Purchasing Experience

Sara begins her journey by visiting a ticket vending machine at her local train station. She struggles to find the English option on the interface and spends several minutes trying to locate it. Once she selects English, the interface remains unintuitive as Sara attempts to input her destination and select a ticket type.

After navigating through the prompts, Sara attempts payment with her card but receives an error. She retries the payment method multiple times, feeling increasingly frustrated. Eventually, she completes the transaction and receives a digital ticket, though doubts linger about its validity.

Later, she visits a ticket counter for a different trip, hoping for better support. Despite the staff's assistance, language barriers and unfamiliarity with the system lead to misunderstandings. Sara leaves uncertain about the ticket's accuracy and its compatibility with her journey.

This experience highlights stress and usability issues in ticket purchasing for international students.
