# Persona: Sara Das

## Basic Details
- Name: Sara Das
- Age: 23
- Nationality: Indian
- Education: Master’s student at the University of Berlin

## Motivations
1. Manage travel costs effectively with student discounts.
2. Reduce stress and confusion during ticket purchases.

## Pain Points
1. Confusion with ticketing terms and processes in German.
2. Errors during payment or input at vending machines.

## Goals
1. Purchase monthly discounted tickets quickly and correctly.
2. Feel confident about the validity of purchased tickets.
3. Sara has successfully purchased a valid train ticket, ensuring that it is correct for her intended travel destination and time.
